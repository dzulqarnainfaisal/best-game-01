NEON VOID: a 2D neon arcade space shooter. Pilot a small ship, dodge asteroids, collect energy orbs, chain combos to OVERDRIVE, and clear all 10 waves with 3 lives.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 in a browser. WASD or arrows to move, mouse to aim, hold click or Space to fire, Shift to dash, P to pause, R to restart.
